Help

How do I save an AI response?

Install the Diary extension, then click the 🔥 Save button that appears after any AI response on Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and more.

Which AI chatbots are supported?

Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Mistral, DeepSeek, Grok, and Meta AI. More being added regularly.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your entries are stored in your personal archive and are never shared or used for AI training. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Can I search my saved entries?

Yes — use the search bar at the top of your Diary to find any entry instantly by keyword, topic, or AI source.

Can I share or export an entry?

Yes — open any entry and use Copy, WhatsApp, Email, or Print (which you can also use to save a PDF, via your browser's own print dialog).

What is "Continue Conversation"?

It lets you keep talking with the AI directly from a saved entry, right inside Diary — no need to go back to the original site. Diary picks up exactly where that conversation left off.

Is there a limit on how many conversations I can continue?

Free accounts can continue up to 20 different entries each calendar month, resetting on the 1st. Diary Pro removes this limit entirely.

Is there a limit on how long one continued conversation can get?

Yes, a per-conversation guardrail that applies even on Diary Pro — 8 new messages on free accounts, 50 on Pro. This is well beyond what an ordinary conversation needs; it exists to bound cost on the rare, unusually long conversation, not to interrupt normal use.

What does Diary Pro cost, and what do I get?

$14.95/month, or $11.96/month billed yearly (20% off). Diary Pro removes the monthly Continue Conversation limit, raises the per-conversation guardrail from 8 to 50 messages, and includes everything in the free plan — unlimited saves, unlimited search, and your full Lifetime Archive.

What's the difference between Diary and Forge?

Diary is its own, standalone product — save and continue conversations from any single AI. Forge is a separate, full multi-AI decision platform by the same team, comparing 8 AIs at once and synthesizing a single best answer. Learn more →

Why does Sync sometimes take a moment, or show "still syncing"?

Sync re-reads the original conversation to pull in anything new since your entry was last saved. Some AI providers render more slowly than others, so this occasionally takes a few extra seconds — it isn't stuck, just working. If a "still syncing" message doesn't clear after trying again, contact us below.
