Help

How do I save an AI response?

Install the Diary extension, then click the 🔥 Save button that appears after any AI response on Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and more.

Which AI chatbots are supported?

Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Mistral, DeepSeek, Grok, and Meta AI. More being added regularly.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your entries are stored in your personal archive and are never shared or used for AI training. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Can I search my saved entries?

Yes — use the search bar at the top of your Diary to find any entry instantly by keyword, topic, or AI source.

What is Forge?

Forge is the full multi-AI decision platform — compare 8 AIs simultaneously and get a synthesised best answer. Diary is a standalone product by the same team. Learn more →

Can I export my diary?

Export functionality is coming soon. In the meantime, you can copy any entry individually from your Diary page.
