What we collect

We collect your email address and the AI responses you choose to save to your Diary. We do not collect browsing history, keystrokes, or any data you do not explicitly save.

Your saved entries are stored securely in your personal archive and used only to display them back to you. We do not sell, share, or use your entries for AI training.

Your data is stored on servers in the European Union and is encrypted at rest and in transit. We use industry-standard security practices.

Cookies

We use a single session cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

You can export or delete all your data at any time from your account settings. To request deletion, contact us at privacy@projectcoachai.com.
