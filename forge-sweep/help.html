Help

How does Forge Sweep work?

Type your question once and Forge Sweep sends it to all 8 AI chatbots simultaneously via the Chatbots API. When responses come in, Sweep synthesises the strongest answer from across all of them — so you get the best of every AI, not just one.

Which AI chatbots does Sweep query?

Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral, and Meta AI. All 8 are queried simultaneously on every Sweep.

Do I need my own AI accounts?

No. Forge Sweep connects via the Chatbots API — no individual sign-ins or subscriptions required. Your Sweep account is all you need.

What is "Best Answer" (Strongest Answer)?

After all AIs respond, Sweep synthesises the strongest, most comprehensive answer from across all responses. You can also expand individual AI cards to read each response in full.

What is "Continue the conversation"?

After your Sweep, you can click 🔥 Forge to continue the conversation with the AI that gave the best answer — your question and their response are pre-loaded.

Where are my Sweep results saved?

Your results are saved to My Archive — a shared diary across all Forge products. Access it from the sidebar or your profile. Each entry is tagged with "Sweep" as the source.

What is the Prompt Library?

The Prompt Library in your profile lets you save, organise, and reuse your best Sweep prompts. Click Use on any saved prompt to load it directly into the Sweep input.

How many Sweeps can I run on the free plan?

Free accounts include a limited number of Sweeps per month. Upgrade to Sweep Pro for unlimited Sweeps, all 8 AIs, and full Strongest Answer synthesis.

What is Forge?

Forge is the full multi-AI decision workspace — combining Sweep, Excel, Documents, and Diary in one platform. Sweep is a standalone product by the same team. Learn more →
